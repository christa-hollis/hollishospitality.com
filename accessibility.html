Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to learn about Hollis, the AI department for hotels, and to get in touch with us through this site, whatever technology they use to get here.

What we are working toward

We aim for hollishospitality.com to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2, Level AA. These are the internationally recognized standards for making web content usable by people with disabilities, including people who use screen readers, navigate by keyboard, or need to adjust text size, spacing and color.

What we have built in

The site is a home page, a contact page and its policy pages, and it was built with accessibility in mind from the start. It includes:

  • Keyboard navigation. Links, buttons and form controls can be reached and used with a keyboard alone, and tabbed sections can be moved through with the arrow keys.
  • Visible focus. A clear outline shows which element has keyboard focus.
  • Labeled form fields. Each field on the contact form has a text label that is announced by screen readers.
  • Reduced motion. If your device is set to reduce motion, the site's animations, including the opening animation, are turned off. The opening animation can also be skipped at any time.
  • Text alternatives. The Hollis logo has a text alternative, and purely decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.

We would rather tell you plainly what we have done than claim a standard we have not confirmed we meet. We continue to review the site and make improvements.

If something on this site does not work for you

Please tell us. Reports from visitors are the fastest way we find problems, and we would rather hear about it than have you give up on us.

  • Email christa@hollishospitality.com

Tell us the page you were on and what happened, and a person on our team will follow up with you. If we cannot fix the problem quickly, we will find another way to get you what you needed in the meantime, including taking your inquiry by email instead of through the contact form.

Parts of this site provided by others

The site's typefaces are delivered by a third-party web font service, and the contact form is processed by our email and productivity provider. If a problem with either one gets in your way, contact us and a person will help you finish what you started.
